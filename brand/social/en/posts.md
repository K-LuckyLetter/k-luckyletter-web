# Day Master series: English posts

One post per day, with the card of the same name attached. Profile link: https://k-luckyletter.com/en

## Opening post (pin first)

```
In Korean saju, your birth day gives you one of ten "Day Masters". It's the character that stands for you in your chart, and Koreans read it to understand how someone works.

Over the next ten posts: all ten, and what each is like at work.

Don't know yours? It takes a minute and it's free. Link in bio.
```

## 01-gap.png

```
甲 (Gap) · Yang Wood: the tall tree

At work you grow in one direction: up. You set the goal first and figure out the rest later. People follow you because you don't waver.

Weak spot: you'd rather break than bend. Changing course feels like losing.

Is 甲 your Day Master? Free check, link in bio.
```
